Daily Health Tips for All Seasons

Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water throughout the day, especially during hot weather.
Balanced Diet: Include fresh fruits, vegetables, and whole grains in your meals.
Sun Protection: Use sunscreen and wear hats when outdoors to protect your skin.
Regular Exercise: Engage in moderate physical activity daily for overall wellness.
Avoid Pollution Exposure: On poor air quality days, limit outdoor activities.
