Clothing Suggestions

Lightweight breathable fabrics for hot weather
Layered clothing for cool or variable temperatures
Waterproof and windproof gear for rainy or windy days
Warm hats, scarves, and gloves for cold weather
UV-protective clothing and sunscreen for sunny days
Comfortable and sturdy footwear for outdoor activities

Guidance

Choose clothing based on the current and forecasted weather conditions to ensure comfort and safety. Consider layering during unpredictable weather and always protect yourself from extreme elements such as UV rays, cold, or rain. Stay updated on air quality and adjust your clothing and outdoor plans accordingly.
