Common Weather-Related Health Hazards

Heat Stroke: Occurs from prolonged exposure to high temperatures. Symptoms include dizziness, nausea, and confusion. Immediate cooling and hydration are essential.
Hypothermia: A dangerous drop in body temperature due to cold exposure. Shivering, slow speech, and fatigue are warning signs.
Allergic Reactions: Triggered by pollen and pollutants; symptoms include sneezing, itchy eyes, and skin rashes.
Respiratory Problems: Caused by poor air quality and pollutants, leading to asthma attacks and bronchitis.
Dehydration: Lack of sufficient fluids, common in hot and dry weather, causing headaches and weakness.
Sunburn: Skin damage from excessive UV exposure. Use sunscreen and wear protective clothing outdoors.

Guidance

Always be aware of weather conditions and air quality before planning outdoor activities. Take necessary precautions to protect yourself from extreme heat, cold, or pollution.

Maintain hydration, wear appropriate clothing, and use sun protection when needed. If symptoms of heat stroke or hypothermia appear, seek immediate medical attention.
